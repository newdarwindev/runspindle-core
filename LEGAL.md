# Legal and provenance status

Publication does not assert that all material shares one license. No blanket open-source license is assigned in this scaffold. Newly authored scaffolding and owner-directed specification adaptations are identified in provenance. Selected third-party source and binary imports remain subject to their original license/notice and content review. Do not infer redistribution rights from GitHub visibility.

When importing upstream code, retain its license and copyright notices and record the exact commit, original path and modifications. Do not apply a new project license over imported material without resolving these rights.

The optional offline authority verifier directly uses the already locked ring
0.17.14 crate (Apache-2.0 AND ISC). Exact crate and notice hashes are inventoried
in docs/runtime-dependencies.json; all three upstream license files are retained
in licenses/authority. No ring source is copied into this repository.
